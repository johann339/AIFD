Style Guide

A bubblegum scrapbook diary system for a playful, dreamy, handcrafted web presence.

Site 1: Sanrio

What I like about it:

  • pastel colour palette
  • cute decorative graphics in motion
  • playful atmosphere
  • rounded shapes with contrast edges

Site 2: Mean Girls

What I like about it:

  • scrapbook aesthetic
  • layered imagery
  • strong visual personality
  • decorative composition

TYPOGRAPHY

Heading font: Parisienne

Body font: Cormorant Garamond

Headings are: medium

COLOUR

Background: #F3B3D5

Text: #8C2D65

Accent: #FF68B2

The accent colour is used for:

  • links
  • borders
  • buttons
  • decorative graphics
  • sparkles

LAYOUT

Columns on a laptop: 1 centered column

Columns on a phone: 1 centered column

The hero image runs: full width across the top

Images should be:

  • soft and dreamy
  • pastel pink dominant
  • scrapbook-inspired
  • high resolution
  • whimsical rather than realistic

Images may contain:

  • ribbons
  • glitter
  • clouds
  • stickers
  • collage elements

Avoid:

  • dark photography
  • heavy shadows
  • corporate stock photos
  • generic AI imagery

My name sits: at the top of the page above all content, only the first letter is capitalized

LINKS IN MY WORK LIST

They are: filled buttons with gradient

Corners: fully rounded pill shape

On hover: lift upward slightly and glow

MOVING BACKGROUND

What it is: floating glitter, sparkles, stickers and decorative elements

Speed: medium

Motion should be continuous and gentle.

Elements may float, twinkle, shimmer, drift, or pulse. Motion should feel dreamy rather than chaotic.

DECORATIVE ELEMENTS

Use:

  • lace curtains at the top corners
  • charm bracelets attached to the card edges
  • scrapbook paper layers behind the content card
  • glitter particles across the background
  • hearts, bows and sparkles around the perimeter

Decorative graphics should occupy approximately 10-20% of the screen and should not obstruct text.

Avoid:

  • dark colours
  • minimalism
  • sharp corners
  • corporate design
  • monochrome layouts

GENERAL FEEL

The page should feel like:

  • bubblegum
  • kawaii
  • scrapbook diary
  • dreamy
  • playful
  • feminine
  • handcrafted

The design should remain readable and organized. Decorations should frame content rather than overwhelm it.
