Design constraints: Each study keeps a two-page book, a celestial setting, and the words BARUCH COLLEGE. The page curvature, orbit grammar, number and scale of planets, star fields, and letterforms can change radically. Both words remain centered on solid, high-contrast page surfaces.
No images from other designers are used in generating these patches. Artwork is algorithmically drawn from the original hand-sketched forms.
