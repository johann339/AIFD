03 / Design rationale

Artist Statement

At the center of my mission patch is an open book, representing Baruch College and the knowledge produced through education. Above it, I placed a large arch that, together with the book’s open pages, suggests the silhouette of a rocket preparing to launch. I wanted the rocket to emerge from the relationship between the shapes rather than draw a spacecraft outright.

The circular border gives the design the identity of a mission insignia, while planets, stars, and orbital paths suggest scientific observation and the possibilities of discovery beyond Earth. The difference in planetary sizes creates a sense of distance and scale. Some paths use dashed lines, like trajectories that are being measured or mapped, while the star at the top becomes a focal point and a symbol of aspiration.

I placed “BARUCH COLLEGE” inside the book itself because I wanted the institution to be part of the image, not an unrelated label. A deep blue background, pale outlines, and restrained gold accents reinforce the feeling of looking into space without overwhelming the original line drawing.

For me, the patch communicates that exploration begins with learning. Baruch’s participation in space research is an extension of the curiosity, experimentation, and collaboration that begin in the classroom.

A.I. disclosure

AI tools were used during the development of this project. ChatGPT helped translate my hand-drawn sketch into an interactive SVG-based generator, expand and test the variation controls, and produce digital patch variations. AI assistance was also used to prepare a draft of this artist statement. The central concept—the open book and arch forming an implied rocket—originated in my own sketches.
